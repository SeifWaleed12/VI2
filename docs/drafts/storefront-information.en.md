## About Vi2

Vi2 is a health and wellness storefront for customers in Egypt, bringing supplements, wellness products and sports nutrition into one place.

### What you can explore

Browse the collection by category, brand or product name. Product pages show the available options and information supplied for each product.

### Information that helps you choose

Review the product label and available details before choosing a supplement. Product information does not establish a medical recommendation or a guaranteed outcome.

### The business behind Vi2

This section will identify the business responsible for the store.

**Owner input required:** Registered business name, legal form, registered address, registration and tax details where applicable, and an approved brand introduction.

## Contact us

Questions about a product, an order or your account? This page will bring the store’s approved support details together.

### Customer support

A verified support channel will be listed here before this page is published.

**Owner input required:** Support email, customer-service phone or WhatsApp number, opening hours, time zone and expected response times. Confirm which channels are actually staffed.

### Help with an order

**Owner input required:** Confirm the process for order changes, cancellations, complaints and escalation.

### Business and returns addresses

The registered business address and any approved returns address will appear here. A warehouse address should not be presented as a public collection point without confirmation.

**Owner input required:** Registered address, returns address and whether customer visits or collection are offered.

## Shipping & delivery

This draft covers delivery within Egypt. Delivery coverage, charges and times must be confirmed before publication.

### Where we deliver

The storefront is intended for customers in Egypt. The final policy will list the areas served and any restrictions.

**Owner input required:** Governorates and districts served, excluded locations and any delivery restrictions.

### Delivery charges

Where checkout is available, review the delivery option and charge shown before placing an order. This draft does not set a delivery price or a free-delivery threshold.

**Owner input required:** Approve the delivery-charge policy, any thresholds or surcharges, and consistency with the configured checkout options.

### Dispatch, tracking and delays

The final policy will explain dispatch times, delivery estimates and how customers receive updates or report a delayed or missing delivery.

**Owner input required:** Processing times, working days and cut-off times, courier arrangements, tracking channel, failed-delivery process and delay/loss resolution.

## Returns & refunds

This draft separates a change of mind from a faulty, damaged or incorrect item. Eligibility and procedures need approval before publication.

### Requesting a return

The published policy will explain how to contact support, identify the order and arrange a return. It must preserve applicable consumer rights.

**Owner input required:** Approved support channel, return-request process and address. Obtain legal review of applicable rights, time limits and any product-specific exceptions.

### Product condition and problems

The final wording will distinguish unopened items, opened or unsealed products, and items that are faulty, damaged or supplied incorrectly. This draft does not impose a blanket exclusion for supplements.

**Owner input required:** Confirm lawful eligibility for each situation, evidence needed, return transport and responsibility for costs. Do not replace statutory rights with an arbitrary store rule.

### Refunds and exchanges

The final policy will explain the refund method, the timing and treatment of delivery charges, and whether exchanges are offered.

**Owner input required:** Refund timing and methods, delivery-charge treatment, exchange process and cancellation handling, checked against applicable law and actual payment capabilities.

## Terms of service

### Who operates the store

The published terms will identify the legal business and how customers can contact it.

**Owner input required:** Legal entity name, registered address, registration and tax details where applicable, support contacts and effective date.

## Privacy notice

This draft explains the areas the store’s privacy notice must cover. The business must verify its data handling and approve the notice before publication.

### Information used by the store

The storefront includes account, cart and checkout features that can handle contact, address and order details. The final notice must describe the information actually collected and why it is used.

**Owner input required:** Data controller identity and contact, complete data inventory, purposes and lawful grounds, including hosting/server logs and customer support. Confirm what is collected in the deployed service.

### Cookies and browser storage

The current storefront uses browser storage for cart continuity and language preference, and cookies for language and sign-in state. This draft does not claim that analytics, advertising or marketing tracking is absent from every deployment.

**Owner input required:** Audit deployed cookies and storage, purposes and durations, consent requirements and all analytics or third-party scripts. Document retention periods and deletion processes.

### Sharing, retention and your requests

The final notice will identify relevant service-provider categories, retention practices and the process for privacy requests. It must describe actual arrangements rather than planned integrations.

**Owner input required:** Processors and recipients, hosting locations and transfers, retention schedules, security measures, rights and request procedures, privacy contact and applicable regulatory requirements. Obtain legal review.
